## Homepage experience board

The four WebP images in [public/moments](public/moments) are original AI-generated editorial illustrations for this demo. The files are `laugh.webp`, `hear.webp`, `distract.webp`, and `cards.webp`. The built-in ImageGen tool generated one contact sheet, then each scene was cropped and encoded as WebP. These scenes depict fictional adults.

Pinterest’s rounded photo tiles and visual grouping informed the layout. [Pinterest Predicts visual playbook](https://business.pinterest.com/pdf/pinterest-predicts/2026-marketing-playbook/) and [Pinterest boards](https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/boards).
