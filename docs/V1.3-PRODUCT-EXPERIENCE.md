# V1.3 Release A — product experience

## What changed

Human Connection now leads with a moment someone wants to have, rather than a directory of profiles. The warm cream, forest-green and terracotta identity continues the earlier branding, with larger readable controls, fewer top-level choices and a consumer navigation bar. No competitor layouts, assets, branding or code were copied. Existing licensed stock portraits remain explicitly fictional.

The homepage asks **“A rough day? A real conversation might help.”** It immediately offers three experiences and two routes: **Find My Moment** or **Explore Hosts**. The proposed value is personal back-and-forth with a human, a style chosen in advance, optional things to do together, host confirmation and visible boundaries. No AI system, social feed, subscription or fabricated success metric was added.

## Customer journey

1. Choose Make Me Laugh, Hear Me Out or Distract Me.
2. Choose an optional style, or **Skip · let it flow**.
3. Select 15 minutes / $10 or 30 minutes / $18, audio/video and English/Spanish.
4. **Match Me Now** requests an eligible fictional host; **I’ll choose a host** opens filtered profiles.
5. On the first request, acknowledge the proposed adult/U.S. scope and the demo limitations. No account or card is required for GitHub Pages.
6. Simulate host acceptance or decline. Acceptance reserves the quote for three minutes.
7. Confirm the exact simulated checkout. Enter the room within three minutes or receive a full automatic simulated reversal.
8. Explore the room controls and optional activity cards. No person is connected; no camera, microphone, recording or generated host response is used.
9. Complete the demo, or leave for a partial-delivery review. Only completed simulations can receive host ratings and optional tips. Undelivered sessions have separate service feedback.
10. View the itemized receipt, request a refund review, save the host, or start a fresh rebooking request.

Back navigation preserves the current experience, style, time, format and language in the running page. Reservation state, selected activities and completed actions survive same-tab reloads through sessionStorage. Draft form values and wizard choices are not durable across a full reload. A resume banner returns to an active reservation. The historical `human-connection-v12` storage key deliberately remains unchanged so existing demo records are retained and migrated additively.

## Styles and activities

| Make Me Laugh | Funny Stories; Playful Banter; Improv & Games | The tiny plot twist; Would you rather…; A wildly unhelpful invention |
| Hear Me Out | Talk About My Day; Just Listen; Friendly Conversation | Where should we start?; Set the pace; A small good thing |
| Distract Me | Trivia & Questions; Unexpected Stories; Conversation Games | A little trivia detour; The door marked “Maybe”; Three clues, one place |

Styles are preferences, not separately priced products. The room suggests a compatible activity and lets the customer choose or clear a card. Trivia can reveal a written answer; other cards explain how real people could use them. No fake participant, automatic counseling, emotional diagnosis or claim of improved mood appears. The production activity panel would need authenticated, shared state and real host controls; the current state is local to one fictional workspace.

## Transparent demo matching

`lib/matching.mjs` filters experience, audio/video, language, demo presence, blocks and reservations. Closed accounts, an existing active request, or paused demo operations prevent a new match. Eligible hosts rank by the chosen style, then fewer requests, then oldest request time, then stable ID order. Counts change only on a successful reservation, including direct host requests. Identical retries do not increment them.

This is fairness among eligible hosts with equal style affinity **within one workspace**, not a global marketplace allocation policy. It uses no AI, random promises, real presence heartbeat or shared host pool. The booking shows why a match was selected. A no-match result explains alternatives and never creates a charge.

## Profiles and trust

Profiles retain search, language/format filters, favorites, availability, rebooking, report and block. They add conversation style, interests, optional preferences and a clearly labeled written introduction. A production 20–30-second introduction clip is described as a future slot; no video of a stock-photo subject was generated or attributed to them.

New profiles show **New** / no verified reviews. Reviews created in the visitor’s workspace are labeled demo reviews, not genuine marketplace evidence. No invented certifications, completed-session counts, vetting badges, response-time guarantees or star averages are advertised.

Host studio and Operations are reachable through **Demo tools** and the mobile menu. They retain applications, presence, incoming requests, net demo earnings, safety cases, refund reviews and journals. Every visitor controls all previews in their own fictional workspace; this is not production authorization.

## Accessibility and mobile design

The layout uses native buttons, labels, selects, fieldsets and modal dialogs; visible focus; larger touch targets; text alternatives; responsive grids; reduced-motion support; dialog Escape/return focus; and view/step focus management. Host cards are stable components, avoiding focus loss on timer refreshes. Customer screens are checked at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 CSS pixels. This is engineering QA, not a screen-reader or WCAG conformance certification.

Safety boundaries remain visible: adults-only platonic entertainment, no therapy/dating/crisis-care claim, leave/report/block controls, no routine recording, and clear notice that demo reports do not notify an operator. The emergency notice directs someone in immediate danger to local emergency services; an approved jurisdiction-specific crisis-resource and escalation program remains a production requirement.
