# V3 product patterns

Reviewed November 24, 2026 using product and help pages. These are public patterns and product descriptions. Their presence gives a design precedent, with effectiveness left for the pilot.

| Application | Observed pattern | Human Connection adaptation and placement | Limit and outcome to verify |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preply | Search filters narrow tutors by suitability, and profiles provide comparison information. [Tutor discovery](https://help.preply.com/en/articles/4179351-find-the-right-tutor-for-you) | Put spoken language and format before results. Keep profiles concise and surface time and total near the request. | Tutoring has different goals and demand. Test accurate discovery, especially when a language has offline supply. |
| Airbnb | Guest and host reviews are tied to a stay and governed by a reviews policy. [Reviews after a stay](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/13) · [Reviews policy](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2673) | Offer a review after the paying guest's delivered booking. In host discovery show separate eligible counts and an explicit empty state. | Review policy and moderation operations need staffing and a clear abuse process. Test that users can distinguish reviews from identity verification. |
| Zoom | A link invites a participant, while waiting-room controls admit people. [Invitations](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0063688) · [Waiting room](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0059359) | Continue to separate the link, guest waiting, and admission in the session. Add host approval before issuing the link because a guest changes a paid conversation. | The two approvals are a Human Connection rule. Verify both denials, revocation and timers before provider testing. |
| Uber | Driver requirements and signup are distinct from the rider experience. [U.S. driver requirements](https://www.uber.com/us/en/drive/requirements/) | Give hosts a separate entry and application, with one shared identity. Use assigned roles and recent MFA for staff. | Provider requirements differ. Test that ordinary authenticated accounts receive no staff capabilities and host applicants cannot self-approve. |
| 7 Cups | Listener discovery offers language filters and explains that some listeners are offline. [Listener discovery](https://help.7cups.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026080054-How-do-I-connect-with-a-Listener) | Discover languages from eligible hosts. Show language supply separately from now-availability. | Human Connection offers time-bounded platonic conversation. Test offline, no-match, declined and expired paths. |
| RentAFriend | The platform describes paid platonic companionship and provider-set hourly rates. [Platonic scope](https://support.rentafriend.com/article/327-can-i-hire-a-friend-to-cuddle-massage-hold-hands-kiss-etc) · [Provider rates](https://support.rentafriend.com/article/382-how-do-friends-set-their-hourly-rates) | State the service boundary and total near checkout and in the introduction. Existing demo prices remain defined by duration. | Their rates and demand cannot establish a price for a 15-minute remote call. Measure realized contribution and satisfaction in an approved pilot. |
| Timeleft | An experience is selected first and matching occurs in a structured event format. [How it works](https://timeleft.com/how-it-works/) | Start with Make Me Laugh, Hear Me Out, or Distract Me before presenting hosts. Keep direct browsing for customers who want more control. | Group social events differ from paid one-to-one sessions. Compare usage and value of guided choice and direct browse. |
