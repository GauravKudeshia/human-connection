## Product-specific tests

- **Clear first session.** Show two groups the same prices, terms and eligible host supply. Vary the prominence of a first-session explanation. Compare completed sessions per eligible started booking, cancellation and support requests.
- **Guided style suggestion.** Compare a catalog pick with a history-based suggestion among guests who choose Surprise Me. Track override and no-match rates along with completion, segmented by history coverage. Treat supply conditions as a confound.
- **Rebook after a good session.** Evaluate whether guests can rebook a host from their receipt or history. Check completed repeat bookings using mature 30-day cohorts. Avoid relying on reminders or nudging someone who had a bad interaction.
- **Invite a Friend.** Rehearse both approvals and rejection paths before considering any live pilot. In an authorized pilot, measure consented admission per issued invite, guest reports, incremental participant minutes and support, and later bookings only with an approved attribution method.
- **Experience-first landing.** For eligible first-time visitors, test experience cards as the primary action against a host-discovery-first layout. Compare eligible booking starts and delivered sessions per consenting visitor. Keep price, supply, duration and access to both paths identical. Compare within staffed time windows to limit the effect of changing availability.
- **Host introductions.** For customers viewing eligible host profiles, vary whether an optional introduction appears before or after the biography. Compare completed bookings per profile visitor, and check how accurately visitors understand the host's style and supported language. Offer a transcript in both variants, with voluntary playback and no change to verification signals. Media quality and host mix can affect results.
- **Trust information.** For first-time customers, test an explanation of host review, cancellation and report controls near the booking action. Measure accurate understanding before payment and first-session completion. Keep full policy access, controls and real availability visible in both variants. An increase in starts without comprehension is insufficient evidence for shipping.
- **Language discovery.** For customers seeking a language other than the default, compare language as a persistent first filter with language inside the secondary panel. Measure eligible preferred-language bookings per filter interaction, plus no-match recovery and same-language completion. Preserve the selected language and avoid substituting another one. Evaluate only with enough qualified supply to report aggregates without revealing small cohorts.

Use staged usability sessions before making causal estimates. Small samples expose missed states and confusing copy. They give imprecise conversion estimates, especially when groups compete for available hosts. [Marketplace experiment evidence](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2020.01157)
