## V3 changes by review

- **Marketplace foundation.** Restored Make Me Laugh through the simulator and sandbox selection. The demo transport covers a complete 30-minute laughter booking at $18. The beta stores declared languages, keeps offline language supply visible, and preserves legacy English and Spanish bookings. Staging review changes use additive tables and require an owned completed session with a captured matching amount. Hosts see separate Conversation Quality and Comfort & Respect counts. Independently moderated text appears on eligible profiles after publication. The host wizard saves drafts, and separate workspace routes use the same managed identity and staff role/MFA gates.
- **Guest invitations.** The public simulator rehearses each role in its own tab. On staging, a host approves before the paying customer can issue a single-use expiring invitation. The guest accepts terms in a separate path and waits for the customer's admission decision. The D1 tests cover concurrent requests and binding, expiry, rejection, revocation, and no additional price or time. Only the first holder of an unused link can claim the waiting slot. Participants can verify the intended guest before admission. Guest audio and video remain disabled. A separate MFA-only test report queue allows staff to inspect simulated guest reports.
- **Analytics and operations.** Added staging style, browse, and language-filter events without storing the spoken-language choice in those event rows. The existing journal stays authoritative, with a separate working estimate for acquisition and additional guest media costs. See the first-call checklist in the [operations runbook](BETA-OPERATIONS-RUNBOOK.md).

## Remaining release work

The first operational milestone is one hosted, invite-only rehearsal using three authorized roles, a vetted approved host, the Stripe sandbox and actual two-device Daily calls. Verify request/accept, a captured test payment, poor-network reconnect, correct receipt or failure refund, review and moderation behavior, a staged guest wait/revoke cycle, and an alert received by a staffed operator. Hosted browser/screen-reader checks, deploy/rollback and D1/R2 restore are separate from the local suite.

Build a timezone-aware availability calendar and atomic future reservations before advertising scheduled sessions. Choose host pricing and compensation, taxes, live settlement and payout operations before any production money flow. Real multiparty media needs policy and provider approval, enforceable guest media permissions, and participant-specific safety and incident workflows. Retention and deletion across providers, real support coverage, supply quality and accessibility require owners and operating evidence.

## Pilot measurement

Use consenting visits as the denominator for visit-to-discovery. Count profile-to-booking against eligible profile visits, and completion against started bookings. Record first completed paid booking, eligible review responses on delivered sessions, paid repeats from mature cohorts, incorrect or failed matches, refund incidence per captured charge, and safety outcomes per delivered session with severity and response times. Host approval uses submitted applications as its denominator. Language supply separates reviewed availability from now-availability and reports unmatched demand. Invite acceptance per issued invite and admission per waiting guest should remain separate. Any guest conversion requires scoped consent and a verified attribution window. Estimated acquisition cost and contribution per delivered session require business records. Use comparable staffed periods and maintain separate simulator, sandbox and approved pilot reporting.
