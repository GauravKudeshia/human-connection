# Human Connection V3.0 full-prompt audit

November 24, 2026

The original matrix audits `d1a6b84`. The [second-pass audit](#second-pass-audit-after-the-fixes) and [latest verification](#latest-verification-for-guest-recovery-safety-and-accessibility) record the later fixes and remaining release gates.

## Verdict

**V3.0 is partially implemented.** The two draft PRs deliver a working public-demo build and a more capable staging implementation. The full brief still requires app engineering, broader verification, and separate operating approvals. My earlier status focused on review readiness and missed significant work that can proceed locally.

- **Working in the draft demo.** Three experience choices, a three-card style picker with Surprise Me, simulated matching and checkout, and a room with activities, a guest rehearsal, and two feedback scores.
- **Working against local staging fixtures.** Eligible-host matching and dynamic language discovery, immediate booking, managed identity and role boundaries, host applications, verified-review eligibility, and two approvals for simulated guest admission. The local suite passed 232 tests.
- **Still to build.** Scheduled booking, complete host search and profiles, fuller host controls, and concrete guest safety and privacy changes. The media adapter supports two participants. Continued guest work includes a gated, provider-tested implementation of actual three-person communication.
- **Public release.** GitHub Pages serves the earlier three-choice demo. The V3 drafts are open and unmerged. [Public demo](https://gauravkudeshia.github.io/human-connection/) · [Foundation PR](https://github.com/GauravKudeshia/human-connection/pull/2) · [Guest PR](https://github.com/GauravKudeshia/human-connection/pull/3)

## Findings that affect the next implementation

| Priority | Finding | Consequence and next action |

| P0 | Guest privacy lifecycle is incomplete. The session-end trigger clears the invite digest. Scope and binding digest, consent version, and report fields can survive the room. Voluntary leave clears the scope and binding, while participant removal, revocation, and the erasure worker lack equivalent cleanup. [Guest schema and trigger](../db/marketplace-guest-invitations.sql#L3-L48) · [Transitions and leave](../lib/marketplace/guest-invitations.mjs#L75-L174) · [Privacy worker](../lib/marketplace/privacy.mjs#L599-L740) | Clear dormant guest credentials at terminal states, define scoped retention for consent and reports, and route reports through an assignable safety case with access and closure rules. | P0 | The separate anonymous staging guest route has basic same-origin POST enforcement, a 1 KiB body limit, no-store responses and a bound HttpOnly cookie. It bypasses the persistent quota used by the signed-in marketplace. The staging flag covers the entire marketplace and the invite UI has no separate control. [Guest HTTP](../lib/marketplace/guest-invitation-http.mjs#L18-L56) · [Marketplace quotas](../lib/marketplace/http-router.mjs#L13-L67) · [Staging configuration](../lib/marketplace/http-context.ts#L12-L30) | Give the guest preview a disabled-by-default control enforced at the UI and server boundaries. [Action dispatch](../lib/marketplace/http-handlers.mjs#L287-L299) · [Link issuance](../lib/marketplace/guest-invitations.mjs#L87-L99) | Make retries preserve the issued invitation's validity and keep rotation an explicit action. | P1 | Replacement, expiry, and same-host rebooking operate on immediately available hosts. There are no selectable future slots, timezone/DST model, or exclusive future reservations. [Booking and claims](../lib/marketplace/store.mjs#L265-L389) · [Browse choices](../app/beta/beta-app.jsx#L930-L1082) | Build durable host availability and atomic future reservations, then expose them on host and customer screens with cancellation rules. |
| P1 | Staging host discovery cannot search name or interests or filter price or date. It exposes inline cards with bios and review summaries, with no full host profile. Auto-match checkout and lobby can omit the host's display name. [Staging discovery](../app/beta/beta-app.jsx#L930-L1082) · [Session heading](../app/beta/beta-session.jsx#L135-L153) | Add a standalone host detail and booking screen and connect it to search and checkout. | P1 | Surprise Me can use positive and negative history for its two current choices. Compatible older style IDs still accepted by booking are excluded from that recommendation pool, and public same-booking repeat clears them. An earlier five-star Improv & Games session alone falls back to Funny Stories. | P2 | Profile views have an allowlisted event without a producer. Language-filter events record experience without selected language, and guest lifecycle has no corresponding analytics. [Event allowlist](../lib/marketplace/operations.mjs#L1-L40) · [Language filter](../app/beta/beta-app.jsx#L852-L857) | Close the profile, application, availability, language-demand, and guest funnel gaps with the smallest necessary event payloads and explicit denominators. ## Research and product decisions

The strongest use of the source material is to design better questions and interfaces. The V3 layout uses a short first-session explanation, structured preferences, optional conversation starters, audio choice, two ratings with separate counts, and straightforward rebooking. Research on anticipated versus experienced conversation quality, responsive listening, voice, trust and choice informs these decisions. Demand, outcomes, and the right number of choices for this app require direct evidence. A corrected 2017 question-asking paper is documented alongside the correction. Competitor notes describe what official materials show. In-app experiments and willingness to pay remain hypotheses. ## Remaining roadmap

1. **P0 · Start with the guest preview's local safeguards.** Add a dedicated default-off guest gate to UI and both sides of the API, then cover anonymous claim/wait throttling, terminal credential cleanup and guest-report retention with targeted tests. Record approvals required for real three-way media and stage the hosted identity, payment, media, alert and recovery rehearsal when authorized.
2. **P1 · Complete marketplace functionality.** Build durable timezone-aware availability and exclusive future slots. Connect full searchable host profiles, clear quotes and pre-join details, editable host information and rebooking. Resolve guest retry/link replacement and deliver provider-controlled third-participant media behind the approved gate.
3. **P2 · Validate ease of use and the product hypothesis.** Complete persona coverage across desktop, mobile, keyboard and assistive technology; preserve useful eligible style history; instrument missing privacy-aware funnels; and run structured first-session and retention studies.
4. **P3 · Optimize measured bottlenecks.** Compare compressed payload and responsive images, profile-query volume, startup and live responsiveness against a captured baseline. Develop personalization, referral and economics experiments when consented pilot data can answer them.
